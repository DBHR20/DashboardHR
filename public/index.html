Evictions

Since 2017, HRO’s mission has been to observe, document and expose the daily state violence perpetrated against people on the move at the French-British border. This database highlights one of the key aspects of this repressive policy: the systematic and repeated evictions from informal living sites in the Calais and Dunkirk area. Even though this policy started in 2017 and HRO started monitoring evictions the same here, here are presented the data from 2022 onwards because of a data loss for the years prior. See the annual and monthly reports to access the data for the years prior to 2022.

Indicators

When a single convoy evicts several living sites, HRO will count it as one "police operation" or ‘eviction operation.’ This is our most reliable dataset.

HRO defines a separate living site as an area deemed unique by its inhabitants, by the authorities during eviction operations, requiring a specific police intervention or a separate phase of the operation. This dataset is slightly less reliable and comprehensive than the ‘police operation’ indicator.

These figures should be regarded as an absolute minimum. They include only those present and observed by our team at the time of the eviction. However, due to obstacles to observation and the difficulty, at times, of counting large numbers of people on the move, HRO is almost never able to determine the exact number of people evicted. Furthermore, many people may be absent (due to accessing services provided by charities and community groups, attempting to cross the border, or leaving early to escape police harassment), whilst still having their personal belongings seized and their living sites destroyed.

Legal basis

'on-the-spot' evictions that are carried out by the police without a court order, as part of investigations into offences committed in the act. They take place on a very regular basis in the Calais area. These operations keep people on the move in constant fear of being evicted.

Operations carried out following a court order are usually large-scale evictions involving the destruction of people's living sites. HRO distinguishes between two types of court-ordered eviction operation. The first type occurs when the court order has been communicated to the residents, allowing them to prepare for the imminent eviction. This is in accordance with French law. The second type occurs when this has not been the case.
